# Results digest (rebuttal experiments, final state of 2026-09-21)

All numbers come from `experiments_new/*/figures*/` and are regenerated by the
`analyze.py` scripts; nothing here is hand-edited. "Tuned" = one shared configuration
per method meta-tuned on 8 held-out LCBench instances (E0); "Table-5" = the submitted
paper's default hyperparameters. Statistics: per-task Mann-Whitney over 20 seeds with
Holm correction ("sig."), cross-task Wilcoxon signed-rank on task means, Friedman +
Nemenyi average ranks (1 = best).

## 1. Decision gates (from the plan)

| gate | outcome | consequence for the paper |
|---|---|---|

| G3 Does the method beat model-based baselines? | **No.** GP-BO wins 25/26 LCBench tasks vs TPE (23 sig.) and 16/19 synthetic tasks vs TPE; it beats FMP on 72 % of synthetic seed-task pairs. ## 4. Sensitivity (12 knobs; rerun 2026-09-20 with LCBench as the primary target)

Design. Two complete studies, each = one-at-a-time sweep (12 knobs x 5 levels + base,
10 seeds) + 200 random joint configurations (5 seeds, fANOVA), on 6 LCBench instances
(7593, 168329, 167185 from the original set; 126026, 167181, 189908 from the added ones;
200 full-fidelity evaluations as in the main protocol) and 3 synthetic functions
(Rastrigin, Schwefel, Ackley+categorical; 500 evaluations). 14 490 runs per study.
* `figures/` : centred on the Table-5 defaults (multi-chain soft FMP, p_dream = 0).
* `figures_tuned/` : centred on the reported tuned configuration (FMP_DREAM); multiplicative
  levels x0.25 ... x4 around each tuned value. This is the study the paper should show.
LCBench score = accuracy minus accuracy of the base configuration, in points (higher is
better); synthetic score = regret / base regret (lower is better). The two are never averaged.

Reading: the tuned configuration is a local optimum for most knobs (almost every
perturbation costs accuracy). Only three knobs move LCBench accuracy by more than one
point: p_dream (pure DREAM, p = 1, loses 3.9 points; no DREAM loses 0.9; the plateau is
0.25-0.5, and the tuned 0.74 sits on its upper edge, 0.4 points below the best level),
n_chains (one chain is best at B = 200; 4+ chains lose 1.5-1.9 points because the budget
per chain becomes too small) and T_mcmc (a 4x hotter chain loses 1.2 points). The
HMM-specific knobs (window, Baum-Welch prior strength, emission scale within 0.3-3x) move
accuracy by <= 0.6 points, consistent with the controller ablation.

Cost of a bad configuration: 200 random joint configurations against the tuned one on
LCBench give a median of −0.2 to −1.2 points on four instances and −3.7 (7593) and −7.7
(168329) on the two hard ones; the worst random configurations lose 8-14 points there, and
only 3 of 6 instances have any random configuration that beats the tuned one. The method is
therefore not robust to arbitrary settings on hard instances; it is robust in a x0.5-x2
neighbourhood of the tuned values (all knobs except p_dream, n_chains, T_mcmc: <= 0.5 points).

fANOVA importance (mean over LCBench instances / over synthetic functions):
kde_tau 0.22 / 0.20, p_dream 0.21 / 0.41, T_mcmc 0.13 / 0.12, n_init 0.09 / 0.04,
burn-in 0.07 / 0.02, wide sigma 0.06 / 0.02, emission scale 0.06 / 0.08, n_chains 0.06 / 0.02,
all others <= 0.04. kde_tau is important over its full random range (0.005-0.5) but flat
in the x0.25-x4 neighbourhood of the tuned value.

## 6. Diagnostics of the HMM controller (tuned runs)

* Observation distribution vs. hand-set emissions (`diagnostics/figures/*/obs_hist_*`):
  the increments O_t are almost never negative; an offline 3-component Gaussian mixture
  fitted to them has components at 0.00 (sd 0.01), 0.10 (0.12) and 0.59 (0.38) on
  LCBench and 0.09, 0.52, 1.01 on synthetic functions. The hand-set EXPLOIT (mean −0.01)
  and TRAPPED (mean 0.015, sd 0.02) emissions are both centred on the same near-zero
  mass and are not separable; EXPLORE absorbs everything else.
* Baum-Welch leaves the transition matrix at its prior (`A_evolution_*`): with prior
  strength 25–90 and a 64-observation buffer the update is a no-op, consistent with G2.
* State occupancy: FMP_DREAM spends 55 % of steps in EXPLORE on LCBench and 76 % on
  synthetic functions; TRAPPED occurs in 0.3–1.8 % of steps and only through the
  rejection counter, never through decoding. Acceptance rate 0.15–0.29 (DREAM) vs
  0.37–0.48 (FMP).
* The decoded state flips between EXPLOIT and EXPLORE on almost every step
  (`states_*_seed0.png`); the posterior is rarely confident.

## 8. What this means for the rebuttal

1. Main text uses the tuned configuration for every method (identical meta-budget,
   disjoint instances) and reports the Table-5 run as the robustness reference.
2. H-MCMC-FMP is presented as an annealed Metropolis-type search with a regime-switching
   proposal mixture; the HMM is the switching policy, shown to beat fixed and rule-based
   policies and to match random switching. The paper must say the latter.
3. DREAM is the recommended kernel for mixed HPO spaces (LCBench: +1.4 points over FMP,
   ties TPE and CMA-ES) and is switched off for continuous benchmark functions, where FMP
   without DREAM ties CMA-ES and beats TPE.
4. GP-BO is the strongest baseline everywhere; the honest claim is "TPE/CMA-ES tier at
   5–8x lower per-evaluation overhead than TPE and 100x lower than GP-BO".
5. The reviewer's specific requests are all answerable with these artifacts:
   baselines (RS, TPE, GP, CMA-ES, BOHB-style), 20 seeds with corrected tests, 12-knob
   sensitivity, emission validation and state trajectories, single-factor ablations.
