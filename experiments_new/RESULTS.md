# Results digest (rebuttal experiments, state of 2026-09-19)

All numbers come from `experiments_new/*/figures*/` and are regenerated by the
`analyze.py` scripts; nothing here is hand-edited. "Tuned" = one shared configuration
per method meta-tuned on 8 held-out LCBench instances (E0); "Table-5" = the submitted
paper's default hyperparameters. Statistics: per-task Mann-Whitney over 20 seeds with
Holm correction ("sig."), cross-task Wilcoxon signed-rank on task means, Friedman +
Nemenyi average ranks (1 = best).

## 4. Sensitivity (12 knobs, Table-5 defaults, 3050 + 1000 runs)

fANOVA importance on 10-D Rastrigin: p_dream 0.47, T_mcmc 0.11, kde_tau 0.10,
emission_scale 0.07, sigma_fraction 0.05, n_init 0.05, others ≤ 0.04. One-at-a-time:
p_dream is the only knob whose effect exceeds 30 % regret (DREAM at p = 1 gives
+30 % regret on synthetic but +3 to +8 accuracy points on LCBench); kde_tau ≥ 0.15
hurts everywhere; the HMM-specific knobs (window, prior strength, emission scale within
0.3–3x) change results by < 10 % regret / < 1 accuracy point.

## 6. Diagnostics of the HMM controller (tuned runs)

* Observation distribution vs. hand-set emissions (`diagnostics/figures/*/obs_hist_*`):
  the increments O_t are almost never negative; an offline 3-component Gaussian mixture
  fitted to them has components at 0.00 (sd 0.01), 0.10 (0.12) and 0.59 (0.38) on
  LCBench and 0.09, 0.52, 1.01 on synthetic functions. The hand-set EXPLOIT (mean −0.01)
  and TRAPPED (mean 0.015, sd 0.02) emissions are both centred on the same near-zero
  mass and are not separable; EXPLORE absorbs everything else.
* Baum-Welch leaves the transition matrix at its prior (`A_evolution_*`): with prior
  strength 25–90 and a 64-observation buffer the update is a no-op, consistent with G2.
* State occupancy: FMP_DREAM spends 55 % of steps in EXPLORE on LCBench and 76 % on
  synthetic functions; TRAPPED occurs in 0.3–1.8 % of steps and only through the
  rejection counter, never through decoding. Acceptance rate 0.15–0.29 (DREAM) vs
  0.37–0.48 (FMP).
* The decoded state flips between EXPLOIT and EXPLORE on almost every step
  (`states_*_seed0.png`); the posterior is rarely confident.

## 8. What this means for the rebuttal

1. Main text uses the tuned configuration for every method (identical meta-budget,
   disjoint instances) and reports the Table-5 run as the robustness reference.
2. H-MCMC-FMP is presented as an annealed Metropolis-type search with a regime-switching
   proposal mixture; the HMM is the switching policy, shown to beat fixed and rule-based
   policies and to match random switching. The paper must say the latter.
3. DREAM is the recommended kernel for mixed HPO spaces (LCBench: +1.4 points over FMP,
   ties TPE and CMA-ES) and is switched off for continuous benchmark functions, where FMP
   without DREAM ties CMA-ES and beats TPE.
4. GP-BO is the strongest baseline everywhere; the honest claim is "TPE/CMA-ES tier at
   5–8x lower per-evaluation overhead than TPE and 100x lower than GP-BO".
5. The reviewer's specific requests are all answerable with these artifacts:
   baselines (RS, TPE, GP, CMA-ES, BOHB-style), 20 seeds with corrected tests, 12-knob
   sensitivity, emission validation and state trajectories, single-factor ablations.
